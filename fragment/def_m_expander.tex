\begin {lemma} Let $\Graph $ be an undirected graph, and let $\Delta $ denote the maximum degree in $\Graph $. Then, $\cardin {\smash {\EV {1}(\Graph )}} = \cardin {\smash {\EV {1}(\Mtrx )}}=\Delta $ if and only one connected component of $\Graph $ is $\Delta $-regular. The multiplicity of $\Delta $ as an eigenvector is the number of $\Delta $-regular connected components. Furthermore, we have $\cardin {\smash {\EV {i}(\Graph )}} \leq \Delta $, for all $i$. \par \lemlab {multiplicity:e:v:d} \end {lemma}
