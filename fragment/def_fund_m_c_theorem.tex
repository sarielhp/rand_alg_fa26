\begin {theorem}[Fundamental theorem of Markov chains] \thmlab {fundamental}Any irreducible, finite, and aperiodic Markov chain has the following properties. \begin {compactenumi} \item All states are ergodic. \par \item There is a unique stationary distribution $\pi $ such that, for $1 \leq i \leq n$, we have $\pi _i > 0$. \par \item For $1 \leq i \leq n$, we have $\MCVstProb {i}{i} =1$ and $\MCExSteps {i}{i} = 1/ \pi _i$. \par \item Let $N(i,t)$ be the number of times the Markov chain visits state $i$ in $t$ steps. Then \begin {equation*} \lim _{t \rightarrow \infty } \frac {N(i,t)}{t} = \pi _i. \end {equation*} Namely, independent of the starting distribution, the process converges to the stationary distribution. \end {compactenumi} \end {theorem}
