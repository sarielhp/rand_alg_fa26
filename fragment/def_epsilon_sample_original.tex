\begin {theorem}[$\eps $-sample theorem, \cite {vc-ucrfe-71}] \thmlab {epsilon:sample}\index {sample!sample@$\eps $-sample!sample theorem@$\eps $-sample theorem|textbf}\index {theorem!sample@$\eps $-sample|textbf}\index {random sample!sample@$\eps $-sample}\index {sample!sample@$\eps $-sample}There is a positive constant $c$ such that if $(\GroundSet ,\RangeSet )$ is any range space with \VC dimension at most $\Dim $, $\FGroundSet \subseteq \GroundSet $ is a finite subset and $\eps , \BadProb > 0$, then a random subset $C\subseteq \FGroundSet $ of cardinality \begin {align*} s = \frac {c}{\eps ^2} \pth { \Dim \log \frac {\Dim }{\eps } + \log \frac {1}{\BadProb }}\^^M\end {align*} is an $\eps $-sample for $\FGroundSet $ with probability at least $1-\BadProb $. \end {theorem}
