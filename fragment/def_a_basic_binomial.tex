\begin {lemma} \lemlab {a:basic:binomial}For any positive integer $n$, the following hold. \par \medskip \par \begin {tabular}{llll} (i) & $(1+1/n)^n \leq e$. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ & (ii) & $(1-1/n)^{n-1} \geq e^{-1}$.\\ (iii) &$n! \geq (n/e)^n$.& (iv) & For any $k \leq n$, we have $\;\;\displaystyle \pth {\frac {n}{k}}^k \leq \binom {n}{k} \leq \pth {\frac {n e}{k}}^k$. \end {tabular} \par \index {binomial!estimates} \end {lemma}
