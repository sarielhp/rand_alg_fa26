\begin {defn} Given a random walk matrix $\TMtrx $ associated with a $d$-regular graph, let $\Basis (\TMtrx )= \permut {v_1,\ldots , v_n}$ denote the \emphOnly {orthonormal eigenvector basis}\index {orthonormal eigenvector basis} defined by $\TMtrx $. That is, $v_1,\ldots , v_n$ is an orthonormal basis for $\Re ^n$, where all these vectors are eigenvectors of $\TMtrx $ and $v_1= 1^n/\sqrt {n}$. Furthermore, let $\TEV {i}$ denote the $i$th eigenvalue of $\TMtrx $, associated with the eigenvector $v_i$, such that $\TEV {1} \geq \TEV {2} \geq \cdots \geq \TEV {n}$. \par \deflab {orthonormal:basis} \end {defn}
