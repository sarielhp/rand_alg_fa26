\begin {lemma} \lemlab {unique:inverse}Consider a prime $p$, and any numbers $x, y \in \ZZ _p$. If $x \neq y$ then, for each pair of numbers $r,s \in \ZZ _{p} = \{0,1, \ldots , p-1\}$, such that $r \neq s$, there is exactly one unique choice of numbers $a,b \in \ZZ _p$ such that $ax + b \pmod p = r$ and $ay + b \pmod p = s$. \end {lemma}
