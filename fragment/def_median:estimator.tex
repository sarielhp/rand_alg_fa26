\begin {theorem} \thmlab {median:estimator}Let $\Distribution $ be a non-negative distribution with $\mu = \Ex {\Distribution }$ and $\nu = \Var {\Distribution }$, and let $\eps , \BadProb \in (0,1)$ be parameters. For some absolute constant $c >0$, let $M \geq 24 \Bigl \lceil { \frac {4 \nu \bigr .}{\eps ^2\mu ^2\bigr .} } \Bigr \rceil \ln \frac {1}{\BadProb }$, and consider sampling variables $X_1, \ldots , X_M \sim \Distribution $. One can compute, in, $O(M)$ time, a quantity $Z$ from the sampled variables, such that \begin {equation*} \Prob { (1-\eps )\mu \leq Z \leq (1+\eps )\mu \Bigr .} \geq 1 - \BadProb . \end {equation*} \end {theorem}
