\begin {theorem}[Radon's theorem] Let $\PntSet = \brc {\pnt _1, \ldots , \pnt _{\hdim +2}}$ be a set of $\hdim +2$ points in $\Re ^\hdim $. Then, there exist two disjoint subsets $C$ and $D$ of $\PntSet $, such that $\CHX {C} \cap \CHX {D} \ne \emptyset $ and $C \cup D = \PntSet $. \index {theorem!Radon's}\index {Radon's theorem}\thmlab {radon} \end {theorem}
