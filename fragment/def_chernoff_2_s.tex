\begin {lemma} \lemlab {chernoff:2:s} Let $X_1, \ldots , X_n \in \{0,1\}$ be $n$ independent random variables, with \begin {math} p_i = \Prob { \bigl . X_i = 1 }, \end {math} for all $i$. let $X = \sum _{i=1}^{n} X_i$, and $\mu = \Ex {\bigl . X} = \sum _i p_i$. For all $\delta \geq 0$, we have \begin {math} \Prob {\bigl . X < (1-\delta )\mu } < \exp \bigl (-\mu \delta ^2/2 \bigr ). \end {math} \end {lemma}
