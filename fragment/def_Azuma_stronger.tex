\begin {theorem}[Azuma's Inequality - Stronger Form] \thmlab {azuma:ineq:s:f}Let $X_0, X_1, \ldots ,$ be a martingale sequence such that for each $k$, \begin {math} \bigl . \cardin {X_k - X_{k-1}} \leq c_k, \end {math} where $c_k$ may depend on $k$. Then, for all $t \geq 0$, and any $\lambda >0$, we have \begin {equation*} \Prob {\bigl . \cardin {X_t - X_0} \geq \lambda }\leq 2 \exp \biggl ( - \frac {\lambda ^2}{2\sum _{k=1}^{t} c_k^2} \biggr ). \end {equation*} \end {theorem}
