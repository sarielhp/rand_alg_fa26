58.1 Some useful estimates

  • Lemma 58.1.1. For any \(n \geq 2\), and \(m \geq 1\), we have that \((1-1/n)^m\geq 1 - m/n\).

  • Proof: Follows by induction. Indeed, for \(m=1\) the claim is immediate. For \(m \geq 2\), we have

    \(\seteqnumber{0}{58.}{0}\)

    \begin{align*} \pth {1-\frac {1}{n}}^m=\pth []{1-\frac {1}{n}} \pth []{1-\frac {1}{n}}^{m-1}\geq \pth []{1-\frac {1}{n}}\pth []{ 1 - \frac {m-1}{n}} \geq 1 - \frac {m}{n}. \end{align*}

This implies the following.

  • Lemma 58.1.2. For any \(m\leq n\), we have that \(1-m/n \leq (1-1/n)^m \leq \exp \pth {-m/n}\).
